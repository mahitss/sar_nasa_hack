# Manifests

Version-controlled source metadata and reproducibility records belong in this directory.

`nisar_kuwari_catalogue.json` is the Mission 002 NASA CMR revalidation. It records queries, footprints, point-in-polygon results, comparability groups, and a catalogue shortlist. It is not evidence that a measurement succeeded, and the shortlisted pair is pending raster quality control.

`docs/science/mission-000-audit.md` keeps the earlier inspection. Do not treat that note as a second catalogue.

Do not place HDF5, GeoTIFF, GeoPackage, or other large satellite products in this directory.
