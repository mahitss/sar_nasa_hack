# Manifests

Version-controlled source metadata and reproducibility records belong in this directory.

Mission 000 granule identifiers are provisional catalogue extracts. They are written up in `docs/science/mission-000-audit.md`. They are not ingested here until a later mission rechecks them against the NASA CMR catalogue.

Do not place HDF5, GeoTIFF, GeoPackage, or other large satellite products in this directory.
